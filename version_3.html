Naples meets New Jersey

You've had pizza a thousand times — but unless you've been to Naples, you haven't experienced anything like Carmelo's. Our mobile brick oven creates that perfect char, bubbly crust, and smoky aroma that only true wood-fired cooking can deliver.

We use only the finest ingredients: San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, herb-infused extra virgin olive oil, and expertly crafted dough. Every bite is designed to be unforgettable.
