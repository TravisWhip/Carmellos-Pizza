Our Story

Learn about the family tradition that makes our pizzas special

Family-Owned Since 1985

Founded by Carmelo DiGiuseppe, our pizzeria has been serving the community with authentic Italian flavors for over three decades. Our secret? A traditional brick oven that creates the perfect crispy crust and bubbly cheese.

We source our ingredients locally whenever possible, ensuring the highest quality in every slice. Our family recipes have been passed down through generations, bringing the taste of Italy to your table.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or just craving a delicious meal, Carmelo's Brick Oven delivers the authentic taste that makes our customers come back for more.
