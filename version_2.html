Our Story

Naples Meets
New Jersey

You've had pizza a thousand times — but unless you've been to Naples, you haven't tasted anything like Carmelo's. Our mobile brick oven reaches 900°F, creating that perfect char, bubbly crust, and smoky flavor that only true wood-fired cooking delivers.

We use San Marzano tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, herb-infused extra virgin olive oil, and expertly crafted dough — making every single bite unforgettable.
