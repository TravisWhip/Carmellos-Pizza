Our Story

Born from a Love of
Wood-Fired Tradition

Carmelo's was born out of a passion for creating unforgettable events and dazzling guests with extraordinary flavors. As lifelong fans of wood-fired, brick oven pizza, we were introduced to the concept of a portable wood-fired oven in 2009 — and we were instantly hooked.

For centuries, brick ovens have been at the heart of Sicilian villages, bringing people together through the warmth of food and friendship. That same tradition lives on with Carmelo's, as we continue to craft delicious, wood-fired pizza and unforgettable experiences — one event at a time.
