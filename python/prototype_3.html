Our Story

Founded in 1985 by Carmello S. DiMaria, Carmello's Brick Oven Pizzas began as a family recipe passed down through generations.

Our traditional Neapolitan-style pizzas are made with the finest ingredients, cooked in our authentic wood-fired brick oven for 90 seconds at 500°C.

Each pizza is crafted with care and authenticity that brings the taste of Italy to your table.
