Our Story

Founded in 2005 by Carmelo Bianchi, our pizza shop started as a small family operation with a dream to bring authentic Italian flavors to our community. With over 18 years of experience, we've perfected our craft while staying true to traditional methods.

Our brick oven, hand-carved from local stone, is the heart of our operation. Every pizza is hand-tossed, topped with care, and baked to perfection in this ancient baking technique that ensures every bite is crispy on the outside and perfectly airy on the inside.

We believe that great pizza is about more than just food - it's about creating memories, bringing families together, and sharing the joy of a perfectly made meal. That's what we do - we make pizzas with heart.
