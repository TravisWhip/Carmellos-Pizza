Our Story

For over thirty years, Carmelo's Brick Oven Pizzas has brought authentic Italian flavors to our community. Our master pizzaiolo, Carmelo himself, brings a centuries-old tradition of pizza-making to every creation.

Every pizza is handcrafted in our traditional brick oven, which maintains the perfect temperature for crispy yet chewy crusts. Our dough is prepared fresh daily using techniques passed down through generations.

We use only the finest ingredients – from imported San Marzano tomatoes to aged Parmigiano-Reggiano – ensuring each bite delivers an authentic taste of Italy.
