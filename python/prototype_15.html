Our Story

Founded in 1995 by Carmelo Romano, Carmelo's Brick Oven Pizzas began as a small family restaurant with a passion for authentic Italian pizza making. We've been perfecting our craft for over 25 years.

Our traditional brick oven, imported from Naples, ensures every pizza is cooked to perfection with the crispy bottom and soft, fluffy top that defines authentic Neapolitan style.

Every ingredient is carefully selected, from our farm-fresh tomatoes to our locally-sourced mozzarella. Our dough is made fresh daily and allowed to rise for 72 hours for the perfect flavor and texture.
