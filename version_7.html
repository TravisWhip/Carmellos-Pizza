We Bring Naples to Your Doorstep

By Billy Yago · Carmelo's Brick Oven Pizza

You've had pizza a thousand times before. But unless you've walked the cobblestone streets of Naples — the birthplace of pizza — you've never experienced the kind of wood-fired, thin-crust perfection that Carmelo's serves from our mobile brick oven.

Our oven reaches 900 degrees Fahrenheit, producing a blistered, charred crust with that unmistakable smoky depth in just 90 seconds. We use San Marzano tomatoes imported from the volcanic soil of Mount Vesuvius, fresh mozzarella pulled daily, herb-infused extra virgin olive oil, and dough that's been perfected through years of craft. We don't do bland banquet hall food. We don't do generic. Every event we cater gets the full Carmelo's experience — custom menus designed around your taste and budget, professional chefs and servers, and an interactive cooking show that your guests will talk about for years. From weddings to corporate events, graduations to backyard parties, we bring the full pizzeria experience directly to your location anywhere in New Jersey and the tri-state area.
